Neural Encoding & Synaptic Plasticity

Total:Marks
Sample Answer4 key concepts highlighted

High-frequency stimulation releases glutamate which initially binds to post-synaptic AMPA receptors, causing local sodium (Na+) influx and depolarizing the dendritic membrane to dispel the magnesium (Mg2+) block from NMDA receptors.

Unblocked NMDA receptors allow rapid calcium (Ca2+) influx, which activates CaMKII to insert additional AMPA receptors into the post-synaptic membrane for enhanced conductivity.

Sustained calcium influx triggers retrograde nitric oxide (NO) signaling back to the presynaptic terminal, increasing long-term neurotransmitter release.

Expected Length: ~180–250 words
